Positioning

For Pokémon players, collectors and the parents shopping for them in Metro Vancouver, Captured! Cards is the local card shop that treats singles like a catalogue, not a lottery. Real scans, tagged conditions, and same-day buyback.

The big idea · Deal Me In

The identity borrows the rituals of the hobby: a hand fanned on the table, a binder page, a price sticker, the shop counter. Things are dealt, stacked, tilted and stamped. Nothing in the chrome is holographic, so real foil is the only shine on the page.
