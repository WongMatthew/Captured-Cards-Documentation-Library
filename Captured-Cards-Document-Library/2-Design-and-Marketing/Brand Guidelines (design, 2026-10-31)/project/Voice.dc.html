Voice and tone

Foundations
1Facts collectors scan forName, number, rarity, condition, price, in that order.
2Short, active, concreteHeadlines are 2–6 words. We’re buying.
3Explicit about moneyCurrency, condition basis and terms beside every price.
4Fluent, then friendlyHobby terms where expected; a parent still follows.
5Play with the ritualDealing, pulling, binders. Never franchise catchphrases.
