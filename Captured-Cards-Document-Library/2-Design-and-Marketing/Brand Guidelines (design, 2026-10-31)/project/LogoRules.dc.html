Logo rules

Identity

Clear space

x x x x

x = height of the CARDS letters. Nothing enters this zone: no text, edges, pills or cards.

Minimum size

120 px

On screen 120 px wide. In print 30 mm. Below that, use the typeset signature.

Placement

Top left on web and banners; top left with the date pill top right on social. Size the logo at 8–12% of the artboard width. Never centre it over card art.
