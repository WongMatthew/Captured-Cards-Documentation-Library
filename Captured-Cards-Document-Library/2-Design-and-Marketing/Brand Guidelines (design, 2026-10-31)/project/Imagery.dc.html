09

Imagery and motion

System

Card scans

The hero of every layout.

High-res flatbed scans of the actual card, 600 dpi, cropped to the card edge, no sleeve glare. Front always; back for anything over $100.

Graded slabs

Show the label.

Scan the whole slab straight on so grader, grade and cert number read. Pair with the grade pill: PSA 10 · GEM MT.

Store photography

Real counter, real light.

Bright daylight, people’s hands, binders, the glass case. No staged smiles, no stock photos, no AI-generated cards.

Do / don’t

Do
Show the actual copy being sold
Keep card colour true; no filters
Let foil shine only on the card
Use stripes while drafting, then replace
Don’t
Use official Pokémon key art or logos
Tilt or skew scans on product pages
Crop off set numbers or corners
Publish a stripe placeholder

Motion · deal, don’t float

Deal-inCards slide up 160 px and rotate into the fan, 700 ms, 100 ms stagger
LiftTiles rise 8 px and tilt −1.5° on hover, 250 ms
TickerLoops 45 s; pauses on hover
Easingcubic-bezier(.2,.9,.25,1.1), a small overshoot
Reduced motionEverything static; ticker wraps
