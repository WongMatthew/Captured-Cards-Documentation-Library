Accessibility, IP and checklist

Applications

Accessibility

Text 4.5:1 minimum, 3:1 for 24 px+ headlines. Never rely on colour alone: stock status is a word, condition chips are labelled. Touch targets 44 px+. Every card image gets alt text in house order: “Umbreon ex, 161/131 Prismatic Evolutions, Near Mint.” Respect reduced motion.

Trademarks and IP

We are an independent retailer. Show Pokémon only through photos and scans of real products we sell. Never use the Pokémon logo, official character art, Poké Ball marks or franchise fonts in our own branding, signage or merch. Web footer and print carry: “Pokémon and its trademarks are © Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc. Captured! Cards is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.”

Pre-publish checklist

Ten checks before anything goes out.

  • Logo is the real file, the right variant, outside its clear space
  • Only core colours plus one accent at most
  • Bricolage for display, Instrument for text, 買取 used once
  • Every card slot is a real scan in 63:88; no stripes left
  • Card name · number · set · rarity written in house order
  • Price shows $, CAD stated once, condition basis stated
  • Text meets 4.5:1 (3:1 at 24 px+); no white on Ember or Mint
  • Buttons ≥ 48 px, real links and labels; alt text on every scan
  • One CTA, verb first; one exclamation mark at most
  • Pokémon trademark line present on printed and web pieces
